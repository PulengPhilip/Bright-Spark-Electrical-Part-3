# Off-Page SEO Plan – Bright Spark Electrical Services

Off-page SEO happens outside the website, so I can't build it into the code. This is my plan for how a real Bright Spark would earn attention and trust honestly. Bright Spark is a fictional business, so I have **not** created a Google Business Profile, social accounts, listings or reviews for it.

## 1. Local organisations that could genuinely link to the site

| Organisation (plausible) | Why it would help their audience | Page I would ask them to link to |
|---|---|---|
| A Polokwane residents' association or community newsletter | Residents need a trusted local electrician for faults and emergency call-outs | `services.html` |
| A local small business chamber or business association | Members need maintenance support for offices and shops | `services.html#small-business` |
| A local property-management or landlord network | Landlords need repairs and fault finding between tenancies | `enquiry.html` |

### Polite outreach message (draft)

> Good day,
>
> My name is [Owner name] from Bright Spark Electrical Services, a three-person electrical team that has served Polokwane homes and small businesses since 2018.
>
> We recently published a page explaining what to do in an electrical emergency and how to request a quote online, outside business hours too. If you think it would help your members, you are welcome to share it: [live URL]/services.html
>
> We'd also be glad to give a short electrical safety tip for your next newsletter.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Owner name] – 071 000 0000

Outreach is not guaranteed to earn a backlink or improve rankings. A link only counts as natural if the other site decides to add it. I won't buy links, use link-building services or join link-exchange schemes.

## 2. Social media post (example)

> ⚡ Breaker keeps tripping? Don't ignore it. Our team can find the fault safely.
> Request a quote online any time, even after hours: [live URL]/services.html#fault-finding
> #Polokwane #Electrician

The post links straight to the relevant service section. Social posts can bring real visitors, but likes and shares don't directly raise search rankings.

## 3. Consistent local details (NAP)

I use the same business details everywhere on the site: in the footer of every page, on the Contact page and in the structured data.

- **Name:** Bright Spark Electrical Services
- **Service area:** Polokwane, Limpopo
- **Phone:** 071 000 0000 (placeholder)
- **Email:** info@brightsparkelectrical.co.za (placeholder)
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 07:00–17:00

A real business would copy these exact details into any eligible directory or Google Business Profile listing.

## 4. Honest reviews

The About page asks past customers to share honest feedback. I deliberately left out testimonials because I don't have real ones, and writing fake reviews breaks Google's policies. A real business should invite genuine reviews, never pay or reward people for positive ones, and reply politely to all feedback.
