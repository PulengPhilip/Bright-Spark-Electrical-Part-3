FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you serve?

We work with homes and small businesses in Polokwane and the surrounding area. If you are unsure whether we cover your address, get in touch with us.

How do I get a quotation?

Fill in our online quote request form with a short description of the job. We will contact you to discuss the work or arrange a site visit where needed.

What should I do in an electrical emergency?

If it is safe to do so, switch off the power at the main distribution board, keep away from damaged wiring and phone us on 071 000 0000. Do not use the online form for emergencies.

Do you work for landlords and property managers?

Yes. We help landlords and property managers with maintenance, fault finding and repairs between tenancies.
